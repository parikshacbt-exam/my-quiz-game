Exam Crash Course & Quick Revision Notes

📜 Moolvidhi & Police Law Sections

  • Section 154 (CrPC): FIR darz karna (Sangheya apradh).
  • Section 41 (CrPC): Police bina warrant giraftar kar sakti hai.
  • Section 300 / 302 (IPC): Hatya (Murder) ki paribhasha aur dand.
  • Section 378 / 379 (IPC): Chori (Theft) ka dand.

🏛️ Indian Constitution Key Articles

  • Article 14 to 18: Samanta ka Adhikar (Right to Equality).
  • Article 21: Pran aur Daikhik Swatantrata (Life & Liberty).
  • Article 32: Sanvidhanik Upchar (Sanvidhan ka Hriday - Ambedkar).
  • Article 324: Election Commission of India.

🐪 Rajasthan 50 Naye Zile Facts

  • Kul Zile: Ab 50 zile aur 10 sambhag hain.
  • Anupgarh: Sri Ganganagar aur Bikaner se alag hokar bana.
  • Dudu: Kshetraphal me sabse chhota jila ban gaya.
  • Phalodi: Jodhpur se alag hokar naya jila bana.

🔢 Quantitative Math Speed Tricks

  • Profit%: [(SP - CP) / CP] × 100
  • Work & Time: Dono milkar karenge = (A × B) / (A + B) Din.
  • Simple Interest (SI): (P × R × T) / 100
  • Pythagorean Triplets: (3,4,5), (5,12,13), (7,24,25).
